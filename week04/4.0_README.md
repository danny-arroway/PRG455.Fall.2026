## Lab Activity — Lab 4: IMU Tilt & Calibration Panel

Build a single-screen M5UI application on the Core S3 with a tilt bar that continuously reflects
the device's X-axis accelerometer reading (via the required clamp-and-scale formula), a live
value label updating in step with the bar, and a calibration switch that toggles between Normal
and Sensitive interpretation of the tilt. No buttons — the bar and label update on their own,
every pass through `loop()`, with the switch changing behaviour immediately on toggle.
